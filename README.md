# Welcome to Waikiki (으라차차 와이키키)

---

> "Laughter is the best medicine, and this show is an absolute overdose of it!" 

## About the Series
**Welcome to Waikiki** is a *hilarious* South Korean television series. It revolves around three young men who run a guesthouse called `Waikiki` to raise money for a film production. Their lives get turned upside down when a single mother and her baby suddenly appear.

![Waikiki Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/8/8e/Welcome_to_Waikiki_poster.jpg/250px-Welcome_to_Waikiki_poster.jpg)

## Why I Love It
I consider this one of the best comedy K-dramas of all time.
1. **Incredible Cast:** The chemistry between the actors is top-tier.
2. **Physical Comedy:** The slapstick humor is perfectly executed.
3. **Heartwarming Moments:** Despite the comedy, it has ==very touching scenes== about friendship.
